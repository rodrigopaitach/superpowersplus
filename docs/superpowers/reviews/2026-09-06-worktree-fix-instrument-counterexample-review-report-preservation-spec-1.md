**Issues (if any):**

- **`## Codebase Findings` — no `## External Dependencies` section.** The spec has `## Problem`, `## Acceptance Criteria`, `## Implicit Requirements`, `## Codebase Findings`, `## Coverage Map`, `## Assumptions to Confirm`, `## Coverage the validation does not reach` — and no `## External Dependencies`. POSIX and bash 5.3.9 are folded into `## Codebase Findings` instead, and `## Assumptions to Confirm` argues they belong there. That is exactly the case the required-section rule exists for: an absent section and one saying "None" must not look alike. Write the section, even if it only restates the two sources.

- **`## Coverage Map` — the section does not conform to the format it is charged against.** [`skills/brainstorming/references/coverage-map.md`](skills/brainstorming/references/coverage-map.md), section "Categories": "All ten appear, always, in this order"; section "The four states": `Clear` / `Resolved` / `Deferred` / `Outstanding`; `skills/brainstorming/SKILL.md:253`: the columns are `Category | State | Where it landed`, with the decision record below the table. The spec's map has none of that — ten invented categories (Naming and collisions, Overwrite protection, …), three invented states (`Decided`, `Decided — unchanged`, `Declared out of reach`), a third column headed `Why` instead of `Where it landed`, and no decision record at all. The consequence for planning is concrete: **not one category resolves to an `AC`/`IR` id**, so nothing traces the interview to the criteria, and a reader cannot tell which categories were covered by the interview from which were settled by the author alone. Four constraints were reportedly imposed at approval; none is recorded anywhere in the document.

- **The report path shape is never stated, and three criteria depend on it.** AC7 requires the reference to state "the report path shape"; AC10's gate resolves ledger links to report files; the `## Problem` section's second measurement exists to justify the naming scheme. The spec never says what the path is — no directory, no filename format. `## Coverage Map` says "Subject segment plus exclusive `-N` suffixing", and "subject segment" is defined nowhere. A planner cannot deliver AC7 without inventing the shape, and whatever they invent is what AC10 and AC12 will be built against.

- **`## Problem` — "6 pairs collide" is not reproducible from the ledger as the spec describes it, and no instrument is named.** I measured it. On the Branch column **as written**, the maximum multiplicity of (date, branch, face, round) is 1 — **zero collisions**. The 6 pairs appear only after normalizing the Branch cell by stripping its parenthetical annotation: the cells are `evidence-model (evidence model v2)` (8 rows), `evidence-model (range validation)` (5), `evidence-model (range validation impl)` (1), `evidence-model (whole branch)` (4), `main (evidence-model spec)` (1), `main (release-notes footer fix)` (1). Collapse those to `evidence-model` and `main` and you get exactly six pairs, all on 04/09/2026 — `evidence-model|spec|3`, `spec|2`, `plan|3`, `plan|2`, `plan|1`, `branch|1`. So the number is right, but only under a normalization the spec calls "branch slug" and never defines, and the count claim carries no command at all (the two counts beside it do). This matters beyond form: the collision half of the design — AC2, AC3, AC5, AC6, IR5, half of the criteria — rests entirely on this measurement. Define "branch slug", show the command, and state that the raw column collides zero times.

- **`## Problem` — "Once the conversation ends it cannot be re-read" is an uncited absolute claim about the existing system.** The measured facts support "the returned text is recorded nowhere in this repository" — which is what `grep -c` and the absent report directory establish. "A dispatch's entire output exists only inside the controller's conversation" and "it cannot be re-read" are claims about the harness outside this repository, with no citation and no source, and they are stronger than anything measured. Weaken them to what the measurement reaches, or cite a source.

- **Two requirements stated in prose and absent from the `AC`/`IR` list.** (a) `## Codebase Findings`: "Adding a column means editing this function, and the assertion for `Report` must be seen failing against the unedited reference" — the test edit and the seen-failing red are a real deliverable and no criterion names them (IR3 covers only `write_points` staying green). (b) The pt-BR measurement's whole point — classify by destination state, never by parsing the error message — appears in `## Codebase Findings` and in the Coverage Map's "Error classification" row, and in no criterion. AC5 requires a distinct non-zero status but forbids nothing about message parsing. The plan traces the list, not the prose.

- **AC3 and AC6 declare `behavioral` but state properties a behavioral instrument cannot settle.** AC3's second sentence — "The no-overwrite property is a property of the write operation, not of a preceding existence test" — has a counterexample that passes every test AC3's first sentence admits: `[ -e "$dst" ] || cat -- "$src" > "$dst"` refuses to overwrite in any single-threaded run. Distinguishing it needs a located range showing `set -C` with no guarding `-e` test, which is `structural`. AC6's "on any path through the script" is a completeness claim over the script's control flow; a test covers the paths it drives, and per [`docs/evidence-model.md`](docs/evidence-model.md), section "The instrument must match the scope of the claim", that instrument does not reach it. Split each into the behavioral half and the structural half, or restate the class.

- **AC5's distinctness requirement is vacuous as written.** "a non-zero status distinct from the collision path" — but per AC2 the collision path succeeds and exits 0, so every non-zero status satisfies it. Meanwhile three genuinely distinct failures exist and only one distinctness is required: AC4 (integrity divergence), AC5 (unwritable parent), IR5 (bound exhausted). Name the statuses that must differ, or the plan will pick them arbitrarily and nothing downstream will notice.

- **Three documents restate the six-column list and no criterion covers them.** `README.md:128`, `docs/README.en.md:96` and `docs/README.pt-BR.md:96` each describe the ledger as "date, branch, which review, which round, how many blocking findings it returned, and how many of the previous round's are still open". AC12 migrates the ledger and AC8 the reference; these three go stale silently, and `scripts/check-docs-sync.sh` will require the two READMEs to move together.

- **The spec as written fails this repository's own link gate.** `scripts/check-links.sh` exits 1 on the current tree: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-review-report-preservation-design.md:121: … -> no such file`. Line 121's illustrative `[review-yield.md](…)` is link syntax, and the gate resolves link syntax. Per [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), section "Writing a reference", a path that does not resolve on purpose belongs in backticks. With the pre-commit hook active the spec cannot be committed as it stands.
