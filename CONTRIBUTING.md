# Contributing

**superpowersplus does not take contributions.** It has no contribution
process — Superpowers' issue and pull request templates were removed rather
than rewritten, because there is no workflow here for them to serve.

**Contributing to Superpowers itself?** That happens at
[obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), under its own
guidelines and its own templates. Nothing sent here reaches it: superpowersplus
is a derivative work, not connected to their maintainers, their review process,
or their release schedule, and a change landed here never propagates to them.

**Something wrong with superpowersplus specifically?** Open an issue on
[this repository](https://github.com/rodrigopaitach/superpowersplus/issues).
That includes anything covered by [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md),
which is inherited from Superpowers but enforced only here.

What superpowersplus adds is recorded in
[`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md). Documentation in
[Portuguese](docs/README.pt-BR.md) and [English](docs/README.en.md).
